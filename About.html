About the Founder

John Momanyi Omeka · Writer · Researcher · Thinker

My Mission

Welcome to Engaging Life Insights. I am a writer and researcher deeply passionate about the powerful intersections of health science, technology, data science, and sociology. I love digging into analytical data to find hidden patterns in human behavior, using sociology to explain how those hidden currents shape our everyday cultural realities.

For me, publishing isn't just about sharing isolated facts. It is about combining rigorous research with real human empathy to make sense of the fast-paced world we are actively building.

Core Publication Pillars

Through this digital publication platform, my research and essays focus heavily on four intersecting modern domains:

  • Technology & Data Science: Tracking how digital systems, AI algorithms, and modern automation rewire human cognition and macro-social frameworks.
  • Health Science & Well-being: Exploring the mental, biological, and psychological health challenges of navigating modern life.
  • Sociology & Culture: Investigating shifting cultural values, institutional pressures, and systemic family dynamics.
  • Relationship Psychology: Deconstructing contemporary attachment behaviors and emerging trends, such as the rise of "Invisible Divorce" inside modern households.
